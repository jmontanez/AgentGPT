# HookDraft marketing plan

## Who it's for

Small creators and solo business owners who post Reels, TikToks or Shorts: they have ideas, but staring at a blank page before hitting record slows them down. Those people spend their time on the same short-video apps HookDraft writes for, so that's where most of the marketing goes.

**One-line pitch:** Dump the idea. Get the reel: 3 hooks, a timed scene list and a caption in about 15 seconds.

## 1. Your own short-form videos (the main channel)

Post the same video to Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Aim for one a day for the first 30 days. Every video shows HookDraft on screen: almost nobody clicks a link in your bio, so the app has to be in the video itself.

Five kinds of video to rotate:

| Pillar | Example | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **App demos** | "I typed one messy idea. Fifteen seconds later: a full script." | Put the app in the video |
| **Hook lessons** (list format) | "5 hook mistakes, and number five is the sneakiest" | List format, best item teased early |
| **Build in public** | "I'm a solo founder, and this is the app I built alone" | Both founder reels |
| **Remake a 1M-view video** | Find a reel in the creator/side-hustle niche with ~1M views; remake it with HookDraft in it | "Remake what already works" |
| **"I ran your idea through it"** | Reply to a comment's idea with a screen recording of HookDraft scripting it | The comment-your-idea CTA in launch reel 3 |

**When a video works, post it again.** Use HookDraft's own "Make 3 variations" button on that video's script and post the variations over the next week or two. Most people give up on a winner too early.

**Call to action:** "Comment HOOK and I'll send you the link." This needs either a DM automation tool or answering comments by hand. Answering by hand is fine at the start.

Three ready-to-film launch scripts, written by HookDraft: [marketing/launch-reels.md](marketing/launch-reels.md).

## 2. Launch sites (first users and feedback)

From reel DfFdUJLlSl9 ("five places to get free users"). Spread these over launch week rather than posting everything on day one:

| When | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Launch week, day 1–2 | **r/SideProject** (Reddit) | People there try new apps and say what's broken. Read the subreddit's rules on self-promotion first. |
| Day 1–2 | **Indie Hackers** | Founders who like trying each other's apps. Post the build story, not only the link. |
| Day 1–2 | **Uneed** (uneed.best) | Small, so the launch doesn't get buried, and it links back to the site, which helps Google find it. |
| Week 2, **Tue–Thu** | **Product Hunt** | Most voting happens Tuesday to Thursday. Launch after fixing what the first users reported. |
| Week 2 | **Hacker News** ("Show HN: …") | Big spike if it reaches the front page, but the audience is developers, not creators. Treat it as a bonus, not the plan. |

## 3. Timeline

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Now, before launch** | Start posting hook lessons and build-in-public videos. They need no link, and they build an audience before there's anything to sell. End with "comment HOOK for early access". |
| **Launch day** | Post launch reel 1 (the demo). Send HookDraft to everyone who commented HOOK. |
| **Launch week** | r/SideProject, Indie Hackers, Uneed. Post launch reel 2 (hook mistakes). Fix what early users report. |
| **Week 2** | Product Hunt (Tue–Thu), Show HN. Post launch reel 3 (build in public). |
| **Weeks 3–4** | Repost the best-performing video as variations. Start "I ran your idea through it" replies and 1M-view remakes. |

## 4. What to track

One funnel, weekly: **views → link clicks or HOOK comments → free script → trial started → paid**. The biggest drop-off shows what to fix next. Few clicks means the videos need work; few trial starts means the free script isn't convincing; few paid after the trial means it's the price or the product.

## Reference reels

- https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfdM34dpiKp/ ("Copy this $30M/year AI app's onboarding", 3 tips)
  - Product: sign-in at the end (the founder's biggest conversion win), more questions (time invested raises conversion), animated screens. HookDraft now shows the hooks before sign-in and has optional niche and tone questions.
  - Format worth copying: "Copy this [successful app]'s [thing]", the founder's own quotes on screen as proof, numbered tips, then a freebie for a comment ("comment ONBOARDING for the checklist"). HookDraft's version: "comment HOOK for my hook checklist", which also starts DMs.

- https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfFdUJLlSl9/ ("Here is how to get free users for your app")
  - Five launch sites: r/SideProject, Hacker News (Show HN), Product Hunt (Tue–Thu), Indie Hackers, Uneed.
  - Closing line worth borrowing: "You didn't build the wrong app. You showed it to the wrong people."
  - Format: animated screen mockups with a voiceover; no face on camera needed.
- https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgJEFCrhgSF/ ("My apps made $17K in the last 28 days", solo founder, 6 tips)
  - Remake a video in your niche that already has ~1M views, with your app in it.
  - Put the app inside the video: his videos got 1.1M views, but only 6,000 people opened his profile.
  - When a video works, post it again and again with small changes.
  - Paywall tips (price right after onboarding, higher price, card before trial) shaped PRICING.md.
- https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgFlq2rP0po/ ("Your app looks cheap. I found how to fix it.", 5 tips)
  - Format worth copying: credential up front, numbered list, best item teased early ("number five is the one nobody talks about"), short follow CTA.
  - The tips themselves (fast "wow" moment, onboarding, tiny animations, launch before it's perfect, never an empty screen) went into the app.
