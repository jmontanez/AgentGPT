## Already reviewed

- https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgJEFCrhgSF/ ("My apps made $17K in the last 28 days", solo founder, 6 tips)
  - Remake a video in your niche that already has ~1M views, with HookDraft in it. The fastest route to views, because the idea is already proven.
  - Put the app inside the video. His videos got 1.1M views, but only 6,000 people opened his profile, so a link in bio alone does almost nothing.
  - When a video works, post it again and again with small changes. Most people give up on a winner too early.
  - Paywall tips (price right after onboarding, higher price, card before trial) are product decisions; see the pull request discussion.
- https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgFlq2rP0po/ ("Your app looks cheap. I found how to fix it.", 5 tips)
  - Format worth copying: credential up front, numbered list, tease the best item early ("number five is the one nobody talks about"), short follow CTA.
  - The tips themselves (speed to "wow", onboarding, tiny animations, launch before it's perfect, never show an empty screen) are product work for the app.
